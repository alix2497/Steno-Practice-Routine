6. Suggested Practice Routine

  • Warm-up (5 mins): Slow repetition on the focus words and number drills above. Focus on clean stroke execution without misstrokes.
  • Pass 1 (Dictation): Play the YouTube dictations straight through. Do not stop the video if you fall behind; drop the missed words and resume.
  • Pass 2 (Speed Building): Adjust video playback speed to 1.25x or 1.5x on YouTube. Practice carrying speed through difficult passages.
  • Review: Read back your transcript against the video audio and check for hesitations or unknown briefs.
